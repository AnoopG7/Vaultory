# Business Analyst

**Date: 22/08/2026**

## Product:

**Product 5: Small Business Inventory and Sales App**

## Problem Statement:

We want to develop a simple product for small retailers to manage their inventory and sales. The product should help business owners understand what they have in stock and what is selling.

## Sir said / told:

* The client had **3 stores in the city**.
* Client has a problem in understanding **how much he has stored in his inventory**.
* At times, the client has observed that **the demand and supply are not in sync**.
* Client also has a problem understanding **what he is selling and how much he is selling every day, every quarter and every year**.
* The product should have a feature to allow **store staff to manage inventory** and also **order products from suppliers if the inventory level goes down**.
* The product should help the client **maintain and track ideal safety stock level**.
* It should also help the client's **sales people monitor and track sales performance in different stores**.
* The product should have an **automated ordering feature** to order from the client's supplier whenever **safety stock is down**, using AI.
* The **AI engine** should suggest to the client **what level of inventory is required in his warehouses**.
* The backend data should be stored in a **database** and the application should be managed on **GCP**.
* Product information in the database should be **masked**.
* The product should give a **monitoring system** that helps to monitor **inventory and sales performance to all senior stakeholders**.
